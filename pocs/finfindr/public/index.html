FinFindr / Vorim / Zeko

Put a signed record behind every operating action.

A FinFindr discovery agent identifies a private operational opportunity. Vorim establishes which agent acted, what it was allowed to do, and the record a client can verify without exposing the underlying data.

Which agent?Cryptographic identity unique to the discovery agent.
Was it allowed?Scoped authority is enforced before the remediation runs.
What holds up?A signed record is portable; Zeko anchors only its commitment.
