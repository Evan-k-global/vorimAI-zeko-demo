Kent HOA / Vorim / Zeko

Make consequential maintenance actions accountable.

A Kent maintenance agent prepares a settlement from private community records. Vorim identifies the agent, enforces its delegated authority at runtime, and produces a signed record that explains why the action stopped or settled.

Which agent?Cryptographic identity is bound to the maintenance agent.
Was it allowed?The agent's scope is checked before it can settle a work order.
What holds up?The approval and outcome are signed; Zeko anchors only a commitment.

Scenario

Ready Escalate is selected: the settlement cannot proceed until its separate approval binding resolves.
